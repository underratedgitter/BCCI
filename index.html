President's Message

Insights from Mr. Kiran K. Majmudar on Bharuch’s industrial roadmap and strategic future.

MR. KIRAN K. MAJMUDAR

President, BCCI Bharuch

"Bharuch is more than an industrial destination — it is a strategic pillar of India’s manufacturing and economic ecosystem. From world-class petrochemical complexes and pharmaceuticals to engineering, logistics, and emerging green industries, Bharuch continues to play a vital national role."

"With major infrastructure corridors and initiatives like Asia’s largest Bulk Drug Park, the Valiya Lignite Zone, and established areas like Ankleshwar GIDC and Bharuch GIDC, our region is strengthening its position as an international export hub."

About BCCI Bharuch

Safeguarding, supporting, and scaling enterprise growth in Bharuch.

Why Bharuch Matters

Bharuch is Asia’s largest industrial hub with strong footprint in Chemicals & Petrochemicals, Fertilizers, Pharmaceuticals, Textiles, Glass Manufacturing, Energy, and Logistics. With expanding national highways, ports, and railway connectivity, a unified institutional platform is essential.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To establish Bharuch as a strong, respected, and future-ready industrial hub through a united and sustainable commerce ecosystem.

Mission: Policy advocacy, inter-industry collaboration, trade facilitation, and long-term institutional development.
